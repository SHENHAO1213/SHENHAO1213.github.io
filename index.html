Research themes and current projects

Summary and highlights.

Neural representation & decoding

Aligning representational drift under movie

Characterizing single-neuron and population-level representational change over 5–6 months, and modeling latent neural dynamics with geometrical alignment to preserve decoding performance across time.

Nature Biomedical Engineering · in press
Neural representation & decoding

Tackling representational instability under image

Developing bimodal models with contrastive learning, GANs, transfer learning, and replay for improved neural↔behavior bidirectional prediction.
AI for neuroscience

Automated longitudinal neuron registration

Building an agentic AI workflow that combines algorithmic matching with retrieval-augmented generation over human-curated decisions for spike curation, unit merging, and cross-session neuron matching.
Chronic electrophysiology

Ultralong-term single-neuron recording

Tracking individual neurons for up to 739 days with flexible neural interfaces to study stable electrophysiology and age-related changes in neural representation.
Neural data resource

Functional neural–behavioral atlas

A longitudinal database spanning multiple mazes, mice, brain regions, curated neurons, and thousands of trials, paired with shared latent-space modeling across sessions and animals.
Computational neuroscience

Time-varying neuronal multifunctionality

Studying how single neurons encode multiple behavioral variables over time and classifying longitudinal functional patterns as emergent, decaying, stable, noisy, or non-encoding.

About

Engineering + computation + neuroscience

I am pursuing a PhD in Engineering Science (Bioengineering) at Harvard University. My training spans computational neuroscience, machine learning, chronic electrophysiology, flexible neural interfaces, and neural data systems.

My broader research interest is brain decoding and generalizable modeling: building methods that can extract stable, useful information from neural activity even when the underlying representation changes over time and across individuals.
